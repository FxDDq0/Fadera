## Protocol information

The OSC address space and parameter ranges used by Fadera describe the mixer's network interface. They were determined for interoperability, from public community documentation of the X32 OSC protocol and from the analysis of how the official editor communicates with the mixer, and verified on hardware where indicated in [docs/COMPATIBILITY.md](docs/COMPATIBILITY.md). No code, graphics or other resources of the official editor are included.
